# Notion knowledge evidence

- Use notion-search with a content query; fetch the matched page and preserve its citations and access limitations.
- Preserve the exact query, page IDs, revision/timestamps and links; distinguish authored claims from your inference.
- Respect pagination, truncation and permissions; a partial search is not proof of absence.
- Knowledge collection is read-only: do not edit content or call destination write tools.
- Missing access or evidence becomes an explicit open question naming the owner who can answer it.
- Read the companion usage guidance for the official server reference and host-specific schema checks.
