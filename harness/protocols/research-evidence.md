# Evidence and refutation

- ⛔ `research-source-coverage` — offer exactly the validated source query
  providers configured or detected for the run. Record unavailable evidence as
  open questions, with the missing information and a person who can answer.
- ⛔ `research-active-refutations` — dropping a load-bearing claim requires a
  strict majority of independent planned verifier slots with reasons and
  evidence. Silence, duplicate votes and uncertainty cannot satisfy the quorum.
- ⛔ `research-journaled-brief` — record source results and examinations through
  the core, then complete only its aggregated brief. Preserve evidence and
  refutation reasons in the replayable journal.
