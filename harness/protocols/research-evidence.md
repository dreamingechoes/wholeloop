# Evidence and refutation

- ⛔ `research-source-coverage` — offer exactly the validated source query providers configured or detected for the run. Record unavailable evidence as open questions, with the missing information and a person who can answer.
- ⛔ `research-active-refutations` — dropping a load-bearing claim requires a strict majority of independent planned verifier slots with reasons and evidence. Silence, duplicate votes and uncertainty cannot satisfy the quorum.
- ⛔ `research-minority-counterevidence` — retain a singly refuted claim below the dropping quorum as disputed and low-confidence, with the counterargument in open questions. Confirming votes never erase dissent; preserve the claim, citations, votes and original quorum instead of inventing a narrowed assertion.
- ⛔ `research-journaled-brief` — record source results and examinations through the core, then complete only its aggregated brief. Preserve evidence and refutation reasons in the replayable journal.
