# Framing evidence and identity

- ⛔ `framing-explicit-unknowns` — require the eight core framing questions plus
  ownership; unknown placeholders name both missing evidence and an answerer.
  Structural validity does not establish factual truth; inspect the evidence.
- ⛔ `framing-safe-identity` — reuse an explicit initiative or completed brief
  relation. Title reuse is limited to the same recorded repository path;
  ambiguous or unscoped matches require an explicit ID.
- ⛔ `framing-argued-no-go` — dropped and on-hold calls carry a durable reason;
  on-hold also records a reopening condition. Closed work is not implicitly
  reopened or duplicated to make the flow continue.

Local docs and tracker publication refers to existing store records. A pending
remote handoff is not a completed write or evidence of authenticated access.
