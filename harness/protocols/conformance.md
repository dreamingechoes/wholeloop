# Conformance evidence

- ⛔ `conformance-evidence-boundary` — preparation starts no model. Installed-core
  simulations remain FIXTURE_PASS with simulation evidence; they never close the
  actual native-client matrix. A live structural PASS requires an explicitly live
  process, exact observed version, successful exit and the required journal chain.
- ⛔ `conformance-fresh-fixture` — prepare a new external fixture for each run.
  Bind source/scenario/core hashes, retain the owned standalone initiative and
  installed-skill marker, and preserve the observed repository baseline. Do not
  resume, retry or relabel a claimed fixture. Inspect failures before starting over.
- ⛔ `generated-distribution-not-guidance` — discovery excludes native-looking
  copies under .wholeloop/distribution, including internal aliases into that tree.
  Installed overlays and actual repository guidance remain eligible.

This scenario checks process/journal structure. It does not establish native
protocol correctness, semantic evidence quality, hook activation, permission
isolation, executable provenance or complete absence of external side effects.
Keep authentication in the client's existing store and preserve native permission
and trust checks. Never promote unobserved behavior to a conformance checkmark.
