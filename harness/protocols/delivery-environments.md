# Delivery environment planning

- ⛔ `delivery-recorded-environment-input` — recover a prepared input only from its original consumed budgeted artifact, exact applied ready revision, preflight and stopped original native outcomes. Lost evidence stays unknown without provisioning or report capture. Recorded readiness is historical only; it grants no live execution or acceptance.

- ⛔ `delivery-original-provision-owners` — bind runner preparation to a distinct original phase before backend preflight. Retain its consumed input, expected identity, actual read owners and original registered operation nonces. Require its fresh resource token at actual admission after preflight. Refuse replay, foreign existing resources and changed bindings; recover or cancel those original owners without renewing authority. Owner closure alone establishes neither readiness nor acceptance.

- ⛔ `delivery-original-provision-input` — capture the complete original provisioning request before validation and asynchronous preflight. Caller-owned replacements cannot change its run, key, recipe, helper or workspaces after the observation; effect admission and resource identity retain that original input.

- ⛔ `delivery-original-provision-control` — configured provisioning captures the original parent deadline, signal and ownership predicate; later replacements cannot renew authority. Recheck after asynchronous preflight and before effect/resource admission and positive results. Bound calls and setup by the narrower original limits, cancel only through the live original invoker and retain its outcomes. Keep service lifetime, historical inspection and explicit retirement separate. This grants neither root producer ownership nor semantic acceptance.

- ⛔ `delivery-environment-recipe` — treat discovery and environment-ledger undo entries as inert declarations. Execute no discovered script, hook or backend action during preparation. Pin selected bounded bytes, explicit command origins, immutable images, logical owned mounts, finite resources and declared network phases; refuse ambiguous or unsafe inputs. A prepared recipe supplies neither authority nor observed readiness.

Use the canonical closed recipe and selected-input record. Historical recipe reads verify retained exact bytes; current-input inspection reports changes separately. Actual allocated workspace binding, containment, capacity, secret-reference delivery, service identity and lifecycle require their backend observations. Retention deadlines authorize inspection, not automatic deletion. Never promote planning or worker claims to semantic QA acceptance.

- ⛔ `delivery-environment-runtime` — provision only under a live scoped private grant and finite issued effect, with exact candidate/recipe/image/platform bindings. Persist resource intent before creation and verify actual IDs, labels, creation identity and container controls before startup. Expose no host, controller, credential, sibling or Docker-socket mounts. Missing capacity, containment, network or secret capability refuses execution without fallback.
- ⛔ `delivery-environment-observation` — observe actual copied bytes, owned service identity, process termination and allocated loopback endpoints. A client exit, requested port, old ready record or name supplies no such proof. Retain unknown/failed resources; reinspection never grants authority or establishes semantic/native/human acceptance. Ledger undo recipes remain unexecuted.
- ⛔ `delivery-environment-lifecycle` — fence startup before owned stop/reconciliation; retain failure volumes and immutable evidence until explicit retirement. Observe the original daemon, exact resource identity and actual shutdown/absence before removal. Preserve partial cleanup, stale-review refusals and unknown reservations. Infer no ledger undo, topic deletion or unrelated-resource authority.
- ⛔ `delivery-environment-private-cancellation` — register actual native clients before private input; cancel through a fixed wrapper's private channel and observe its whole group absent. Recovery never signals stored PIDs, treats reused/unobservable identities as unknown and never retroactively invents tracking for incomplete old startup. Serialize cleanup controllers and inspect lost effects before continuation.
