# 0129 — Approved definition revision reservation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## Context

An unattended definition produces a proposed delivery-plan revision before its framing, specification and scopes have independent decisions. Preparing that immutable revision during production reserves its slot even when the proposal is rejected. The root runner already supports adoption of an envelope-authorized definition result, but artifact production alone cannot supply approval or authorize adoption.

## Decision

Add an internal RunnerDefinitionStages component. Require an explicitly unattended policy, active envelope authority and every route-required delegation before production. Exact-plan authority cannot acquire derivation permission. Reuse the original read-only artifact producer and require a complete definition in the captured next-revision proposal before judgment dispatch.

Extend private version-two decision bindings to definition proposals. Bind the exact original producer invokers, deadline, captured artifact, proposal digest and next revision. Every required independent decision must cite that same artifact. Retain all existing request, rubric, source, provenance and outcome checks. Implementation definitions require framing go, specification acceptance and scope acceptance; research definitions retain their existing route-specific research-plan decision. Do not broaden policy or fabricate human approval.

Only after all original independent decisions are current, supported and host-observed may the fixed component prepare the exact immutable revision and return its derived-plan locator. Root engine adoption remains a separate existing operation under the original envelope, grant and deadline. The subsequent plan stage still requires its own existing delivery-plan decision. Do not repeatedly produce or adopt another revision during that stage.

Recover an interrupted reservation from the original captured artifact and published decisions, including while paused, without selecting context or launching a producer or judgment again. An already identical prepared revision is reused; a conflicting immutable revision refuses. Rejection, incomplete definitions, unknown ownership and fixture-only observations cannot reserve the next revision. Cancellation addresses only the original private scope, including after revocation. Public delivery schemas and historical private version-one readers remain unchanged.

## Alternatives rejected

Preparing before judgment prevents correction after rejection. Treating production as approval skips three distinct implementation decisions. Deriving from an exact-plan grant expands authority. Having each plan stage produce and adopt a new revision creates an endless planning loop. Replaying workers to reconstruct a lost reservation consumes more authority and obscures the original outcome. Resetting a deadline on adoption buys unapproved runtime.

## Verification

Directed controls cover undelegated and exact-plan admission, cancellation, fixture production, incomplete definitions, an omitted required decision, a different artifact cited by one decision and persisted multi-decision intent before host construction. Retain builder-plan, original decision, artifact and frozen-contract regressions. A separate finite model-free evaluation uses actual synthetic processes and disposable Git/store fixtures to exercise all three judgments and challenges, rejection, interrupted revision reservation, paused recovery and same-grant/deadline adoption. These normalized observations do not certify real models, authentication, containment or complete-route delivery. Record actual results in the journal.

The final seven-file regression passes 482 cases. Both complete seven-process scenarios pass on macOS Node 24 and Linux Node 20.0.0 with the same bundled candidate. Preserve the corrected Markdown index-column failure; lint, types, build, docs, harness, Markdown, Mermaid and offline package checks pass. Full repository-suite results remain those of the preceding criterion-verification increment.
